# FoodMadeEasy · prototype

**Try it:** https://toiletcoding.github.io/FoodMadeEasy-prototype/

A clickable prototype of FoodMadeEasy, a planned mobile app that turns your calorie and macro targets, food preferences, weekly budget and supermarket offers into a weekly meal plan, a shopping list grouped by store, and a guided meal-prep session.

## Install it on your phone

- **iPhone (Safari):** open the link → Share button → **Add to Home Screen**.
- **Android (Chrome):** open the link → ⋮ menu → **Add to Home screen** / **Install app**.

It then opens full screen from its own icon and works offline. It updates itself: close and reopen it to get the newest version.

Testing tools (skip days ahead, simulate offline, force errors, reset) are under **Profile → ⚙ Settings → Prototype tools**.

## Please note

- This is a prototype for testing the user flow, not the real app.
- All prices, offers, recipes and accounts are made up. FoodMadeEasy is not affiliated with any supermarket shown.
- Nothing leaves your device: your "account" and plans are stored only in your browser.
